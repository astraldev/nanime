## Try it

The numbered controls work. The greyed ones fill out the page and do
nothing.

1. **Scroll the route panel** sideways. The car follows the road and turns
   with every bend, and a trail draws in behind it. It reverses when you
   scroll back. Each stop's marker fills in as the car reaches it.
2. **Click a stop card** to scroll the panel to that stop, and the car
   drives there. The card of the last stop reached is highlighted, and a
   check pops in on every stop the car has passed.
3. **Book trip** runs the booking. The status line scrambles into each
   step's name next to a spinner, and finished steps slide up into the log.
   Pause stops the spinner and the step timer, and Resume carries on from
   the same point. A check pops in when the booking is done.

Reset in the title bar scrolls the route back to the start, cancels the
booking and plays the title in again.

## Built with

- [`useAnimeScroll`](/composables/use-anime-scroll): the panel's
  horizontal scroll, driving the car and the trail
- [`useAnimate`](/composables/use-animate): the car on its motion path, the
  trail, and the title's letters rising into place
- `createMotionPath` and `createDrawable` from
  [`#nanime/proxies/svg`](/composables/utilities#proxies): the car's path
  and heading, and the trail's line drawing
- [`useSplitText`](/composables/use-split-text): the trip title, split into
  letters
- [`useScrambleText`](/composables/use-scramble-text): the booking status
  line
- [`useWaapiAnimate`](/composables/use-waapi-animate): the spinner
- [`<AnimeTransitionGroup>`](/components/transitions): the booking log
- [`<AnimeTransition>`](/components/transitions): the stop checks, the
  spinner swapping to a check, and the paused badge
- `spring` from [`#nanime/easings`](/composables/utilities) and `stagger`
  from [`#nanime/utils`](/composables/utilities)

::collapsible{name="How it's built"}
**Layout.** The window is split into a header, the route panel, the stop
cards and the booking summary. The route panel reports its scroll progress
to the parent, and the stop cards read it. The parent also calls the route
panel to scroll to a stop, and resets the booking and the header.

**Route.** The road is one SVG path, drawn four times: a wide edge, the
asphalt, a dashed centre line and a trail. The car is an SVG group moved
along the asphalt path by [`useAnimate`](/composables/use-animate) with
`createMotionPath(path)`. The proxy returns `translateX`, `translateY` and
`rotate` values that follow the path, so the car turns with each bend. The
trail is the same path wrapped in `createDrawable`, animated with
`draw: ['0 0', '0 1']`.

Neither animation plays on a clock.
[`useAnimeScroll`](/composables/use-anime-scroll) creates a scroll
observer on the panel with `axis: 'x'`, `container` set to the panel and
`target` set to the wide scene inside it. Passing that observer as each
animation's `autoplay` ties the animation's progress to the scroll
position. `sync: true` keeps it locked there with no smoothing, and the
`ease: 'linear'` on the car keeps its position proportional to the scroll.
Each animation gets its own observer, since an observer drives one
animation.

**Stops.** Each stop has an `at` value, the fraction of the road where it
sits. On mount, `getPointAtLength` on the road path places a marker at
each fraction. The car is at the same fraction of the road as the panel is
of its scroll, so a stop is reached when the observer's `progress` passes
its `at`. Clicking a card scrolls the panel to `at` times its scroll range
with `scrollTo({ behavior: 'smooth' })`, and the scroll observer moves the
car there. The check on each card sits in an
[`<AnimeTransition>`](/components/transitions) with a small `spring()`
pop, so it appears as the car passes the stop and leaves when it reverses
past it.

**Title.** [`useSplitText`](/composables/use-split-text) splits the title
into letters with `wrap: 'clip'`, so each letter sits in a clipped box.
[`useAnimate`](/composables/use-animate) moves each letter from `y: '100%'`
to `0` with `stagger(18)`, so they rise into place one after another. The
header is keyed on a counter that Reset increments, which remounts it and
plays the title again.

**Booking.** A `useBooking` composable steps through a list of steps on a
`setTimeout`. Each step writes its text to the status line, which
[`useScrambleText`](/composables/use-scramble-text) watches, so a new text
starts a new scramble. The wait for each step is the scramble's own length
plus the step's delay, so the text always finishes resolving before the
next one starts. Pause clears the timer and Resume schedules the next step
again.

The spinner turns with
[`useWaapiAnimate`](/composables/use-waapi-animate), a looping `rotate` on
the compositor thread, so it keeps turning while the main thread works.
Pause and Resume call its `pause()` and `play()`, so it stops at its
current angle and continues from there. The last three finished steps show
in an [`<AnimeTransitionGroup>`](/components/transitions). New steps fade
up, and the oldest fades out as the rest move up. The spinner, the final
check and the idle caret swap inside an `<AnimeTransition mode="out-in">`.
