Scroll the route panel sideways and the car follows the road. Click a stop
card to drive there, or book the trip and watch the log run. The numbered
controls work, and the greyed ones are decoration.

## Built with

- [`useAnimeScroll`](/composables/use-anime-scroll): the panel's
  horizontal scroll, driving the car and the trail
- [`useAnimate`](/composables/use-animate): the car on its motion path, the
  trail, and the title's letters rising into place
- `createMotionPath` and `createDrawable` from
  [`#nanime/proxies/svg`](/composables/utilities#use-proxies-inside-animation-parameters): the car's path
  and heading, and the trail's line drawing
- [`useSplitText`](/composables/use-split-text): the trip title, split into
  letters
- [`useScrambleText`](/composables/use-scramble-text): the booking status
  line
- [`useWaapiAnimate`](/composables/use-waapi-animate): the spinner
- [`<AnimeTransitionGroup>`](/components/transition): the booking log
- [`<AnimeTransition>`](/components/transition): the stop checks, the
  spinner swapping to a check, and the paused badge
- `spring` from [`#nanime/easings`](/composables/utilities) and `stagger`
  from [`#nanime/utils`](/composables/utilities)

## Scroll drives the car

The road is one SVG path. `createMotionPath` returns the `translateX`,
`translateY` and `rotate` values that follow it, so the car turns with each
bend. Passing a scroll observer as `autoplay` ties progress to the scroll
position, and `sync: true` keeps it locked there with no smoothing.

```ts
const carScroll = useAnimeScroll({
  container: viewport,
  target: scene,
  axis: 'x',
  sync: true,
})

useAnimate(car, () => ({
  ...createMotionPath(road.value),
  ease: 'linear',
  autoplay: carScroll,
}))
```

An observer drives one animation, so the trail gets its own. It wraps the
same path in `createDrawable` and animates `draw: ['0 0', '0 1']`.

## Stops are fractions of the road

Each stop has an `at` value. A stop is reached when the observer's
`progress` passes it, and clicking a card scrolls the panel to `at` times
its scroll range. The car moves because the scroll moves, so the click
needs no animation of its own.

## The booking waits for the scramble

Each step writes its text to the status line, and
[`useScrambleText`](/composables/use-scramble-text) starts a new scramble
for it. The wait before the next step is the scramble's own length plus the
step's delay, so a text finishes resolving before the next one begins.

::collapsible{name="More detail"}
**Layout.** The route panel reports its scroll progress to the parent, and
the stop cards read it. The parent also calls the route panel to scroll to
a stop, and resets the booking and the header.

**Road layers.** The same path is drawn four times: a wide edge, the
asphalt, a dashed centre line and the trail.

**Stop markers.** On mount, `getPointAtLength` on the road path places a
marker at each `at` fraction. The check on each card sits in an
[`<AnimeTransition>`](/components/transition) with a small `spring()`
pop, so it appears as the car passes and leaves when it reverses past.

**Title.** [`useSplitText`](/composables/use-split-text) splits the title
into letters with `wrap: 'clip'`, and [`useAnimate`](/composables/use-animate)
moves each from `y: '100%'` to `0` with `stagger(18)`. The header is keyed
on a counter that Reset increments, which remounts it and plays the title
again.

**Pause and resume.** Pause clears the booking timer and Resume schedules
the next step again. The spinner is a looping `rotate` from
[`useWaapiAnimate`](/composables/use-waapi-animate) on the compositor
thread, so it keeps turning while the main thread works. `pause()` and
`play()` stop it at its current angle and continue from there.

**Booking log.** The last three finished steps show in an
[`<AnimeTransitionGroup>`](/components/transition). New steps fade up and
the oldest fades out as the rest move up. The spinner, the final check and
the idle caret swap inside an `<AnimeTransition mode="out-in">`.
