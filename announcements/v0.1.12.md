## Discord (1426)

**nanime v0.1.12 is out** 🎬

It's Anime.js v4 wrapped as Nuxt composables, so you animate without the `onMounted` guard, the cleanup on unmount, or the SSR hydration worry. Point one at a ref and you're done:

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
const box = useTemplateRef('box')

useAnimate(box, {
  translateX: 200,
  scale: [0.9, 1],
  ease: 'outExpo',
  duration: 800,
})
</script>
```

There are seven of them. `useAnimate` covers CSS, transforms, SVG and plain JS objects. `useWaapiAnimate` hands the work to the browser's compositor, so a modal keeps its frame rate while the main thread is busy hydrating. `useAnimatable` is the one for cursor tracking and drag, where rebuilding an animation on every pointer event would wreck you. Then `useAnimeTimeline` to sequence and scrub, `useSplitText` and `useScrambleText` for text, and `useDraggable` with snapping, bounds and spring release.

This release drops VueUse from the install, so it's just nanime and animejs now. Draggable and timeline methods also became callable before mount, with early calls buffered and replayed once the instance exists, and there are SVG proxies for `morphTo`, `createMotionPath` and `createDrawable`.

If you'd rather poke at something than read: four full examples with source, including a card you drag into a bin, a timeline you can scrub, and text that scatters away from your cursor.

https://nanimejs.netlify.app/examples/introduction
